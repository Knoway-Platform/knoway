# Knoway

**Share ideas. Explore perspectives. See what holds up.**

Knoway is a social platform for discovering content, sharing ideas, and having conversations about the claims within a post.

Share an everyday observation, tell a story, or develop a longer argument. Readers can discuss the whole piece or focus on a specific claim, without losing its original context. When a post includes a prediction, they can take a position and return to see how it turned out.

## Why Knoway

Sometimes the most interesting part of a post is a single sentence—the argument you question, the perspective you hadn't considered, or the prediction you want to revisit. That idea deserves a conversation of its own.

Knoway keeps content, claims, and their discussions connected. For predictions with a clear outcome, that connection extends over time, creating a record of what people said and how it held up.

Not every idea needs a verdict. Stories, opinions, and open questions belong here too. Our goal is to make thoughtful contributions and a record of good judgment matter more in what people discover and whom they trust.

## The experience

### Find something worth reading

Explore a personalized feed of short updates and longer posts. Follow people you value, discover new voices, and encounter perspectives beyond your usual interests.

We're building recommendations that balance relevance, quality, and discovery, with reader feedback helping shape what appears next.

### Write in your own voice

Share a thought, an experience, or an argument. Predictions are optional—not a requirement for contributing.

Posts that include verifiable predictions are called **Ranked posts**. They can still contain stories, opinions, and open questions; only the selected predictions are evaluated against an outcome.

AI helps authors identify potential predictions and clarify how they can be evaluated. Authors choose which to include, and each remains linked to the passage it comes from.

### Give a claim its own conversation

Discuss a post as a whole, or go directly to the claim that caught your attention. Read it in context, examine the reasoning, and join a focused discussion while staying connected to the wider conversation.

### Build a record over time

Readers can agree or disagree with a prediction using in-app points, not real money.

As predictions are resolved, outcomes contribute to a visible track record alongside a person's posts and participation. The aim is to give readers more to go on than how persuasive someone sounds in a single post.
